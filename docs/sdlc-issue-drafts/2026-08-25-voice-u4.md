### Objective

Capture the operator's speech on a toggle, transcribe it through the declared
hosted speech-to-text provider, and leave no audio, no transcript log, and no
telemetry behind.

**Unit U4 · lane G2 · depends on U1.**
**Requirements owned: R10, R12, R25, R26, R27.** These requirements belong to this unit alone; no
other unit in the run implements them.

Parent contract: infiquetra/infiquetra-agent-plugins#27

### Intent

Recording is toggled: one press starts, a second press stops (R10). Version one does
not implement press-and-hold, which would require key-release forwarding that is
unverified and deliberately untested.

Nothing is transcribed and nothing is sent until the operator explicitly stops the
recording (R12). An abandoned recording produces no transcription request at all.

Temporary audio is deleted immediately after transcription, including when
transcription fails (R25). `voice` keeps no transcript log of its own — the only
durable record is the bound agent's own conversation history after the text is
delivered (R26) — and emits no telemetry (R27).

Transcription is hosted, which makes egress external by design. The provider's egress
class must be stated per R21, and the operator's provider choice is a parked decision
recorded on the parent contract.

**Proportionality.** `voice` is a private, single-user developer tool. Prefer the
smallest compatible implementation. Standard-library Python at the repository floor
of `>=3.12`, tested with `unittest` (R31). Do not add multi-tenant, high-availability,
regulatory, or over-defensive machinery. Do not relax credential, shell, filesystem,
Git, privacy, destructive-action, or production-safety boundaries.

### Out-of-scope / non-goals

- Delivering the transcript to the agent — U5 owns delivery and the blocked-state refusal.
- The recording indicator and barge-in behaviour — U6 owns pane presentation.
- Bundling a speech-to-text implementation or managing credentials.
- Continuous listening, wake words, or any background listener.
