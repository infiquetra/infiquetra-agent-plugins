## Why this cannot be resolved inside U2

1. The unit's non-goals forbid moving any other deferred item out of
   `DEFERRED.md`; `models.json`, `tier_resolver.py`, and `tier_policy.json`
   are all deferred items.
2. `fleet_commons_shim.py` can never enter the slice under the portable
   model, and a test-only stub would create a second, divergent writable
   source against the package's byte-copy/provenance mechanism.
3. KTD1's transforms cover only the two entrypoint shapes; the shim import
   inside a bundled module is a shape neither the plan nor the bundle child
   (U5, two-module bundle) accounts for.
